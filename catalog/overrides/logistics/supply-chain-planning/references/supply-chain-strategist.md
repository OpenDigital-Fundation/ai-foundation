# Supply Chain Strategist — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `msitarzewski/agency-agents` at `b859c51`, `specialized/supply-chain-strategist.md#inventory-management-strategies,tco-total-cost-of-ownership-analysis,supply-chain-risk-assessment,multi-source-procurement-strategy,workflow`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Reconcile demand, lead-time observations, stock, unit costs, service definition and supplier capacity before proposing inventory policy. Compare replenishment models under their assumptions; demand variability and lead-time variability must use compatible units. Validate finite/bounded inputs and practical constraints rather than copying a universal safety-stock formula.

Compare total lifecycle cost and concentration/disruption scenarios. Qualify alternatives against actual capacity, recovery time and cost; fixed supplier counts, allocation percentages or generic credit thresholds are not validated policy. Keep trade, contract, hedging and sourcing commitments with responsible owners. Deliver assumptions, feasible scenarios, sensitivity/backtest results and a review cadence.
