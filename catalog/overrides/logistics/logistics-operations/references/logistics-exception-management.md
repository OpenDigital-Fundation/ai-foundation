# logistics-exception-management — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `affaan-m/ECC` at `b38be6a`, `skills/logistics-exception-management/SKILL.md#exception-taxonomy,severity-classification,priority-sequencing,tone-calibration`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Record shipment, promised versus observed milestones, goods/handling requirements and evidence. Classify delay, loss, damage, shortage, refusal or misdelivery without deciding liability from a generic label. Capture photos, custody and temperature evidence where relevant and lawfully available.

Prioritize actual safety, time and commercial impact using the operator's policy. Confirm claim/notice windows and liability in applicable contracts and rules; no universal five-day or dollar threshold is bundled. Escalate safety-sensitive cargo to qualified operators. Communicate confirmed status and approved actions without inventing ETA, promising refunds or naming a party at fault without evidence.
