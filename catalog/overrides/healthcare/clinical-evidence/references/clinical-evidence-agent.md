# Clinical Evidence Agent — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `msitarzewski/agency-agents` at `b859c51`, `healthcare/healthcare-clinical-evidence-agent.md#validated-vs-unvalidated-claims-framework,evidence-synthesis-workflow`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Maintain a claim ledger with source, date/version, population, design, outcome, estimate/uncertainty and applicability. Distinguish published findings, internal observations, extrapolations and unsupported claims. A peer-reviewed paper or clinician sign-off alone does not make a claim universally validated or suitable for unqualified marketing.

For each important claim, inspect the underlying source and limitations, reconcile conflicting evidence and record what was not found. Have the accountable professional review applicability. Do not invent citations, infer patient-specific treatment or import investor/credential claims into clinical evidence. Keep evidence strength, recommendation strength and regulatory status distinct.
