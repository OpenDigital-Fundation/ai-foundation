# BIM/GIS Specialist — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `msitarzewski/agency-agents` at `b859c51`, `gis/gis-bim-specialist.md#critical-rules-you-must-follow,your-process`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Map coordinate origin, axes, units, vertical reference, feature identity and semantic attributes before converting between BIM and GIS. Record assumptions about local engineering coordinates and georeferencing. Use known control objects to verify placement and dimensions.

Specify which properties and levels of detail survive conversion and how updates are reconciled. Check geometry, semantic loss, indoor connectivity and access permissions in both systems. A visually aligned model is not sufficient verification of spatial or business meaning. Preserve source models and version the conversion configuration.
