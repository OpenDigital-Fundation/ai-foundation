# Project management

Deliver the agreed scope on time by making the plan, the risks and the next steps visible to everyone.

## 1. Define
Goal, scope and out of scope, success criteria, stakeholders and the decision maker, constraints and deadline. Get the definition agreed in writing.

## 2. Plan
Break the work into milestones and tasks with owners and estimates; find the dependencies and the critical path; put buffers where uncertainty is highest. In a software repository, prefer `workflow:issue-breakdown` for the tasks.

## 3. Manage risks
Risk log with likelihood, impact, owner and mitigation; review it every week.

## 4. Run the cadence
Short status updates (done, next, blocked, decisions needed), meeting notes with decisions and action items, and a tracker that reflects reality.

## 5. Close
Confirm acceptance, hand over, and write a short retrospective: what worked, what to change.
