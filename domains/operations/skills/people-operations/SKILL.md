# People operations

Hire, onboard and grow people with clear, fair and repeatable processes.

## 1. Hiring
Role scorecard (outcomes, competencies, level) before the job description; structured interview loop where each interviewer owns specific competencies with written rubrics; debrief on evidence, not impressions.

## 2. Onboarding
A 30-60-90 day plan: accounts and equipment ready on day one, a buddy, the first meaningful task in week one, explicit expectations and check-ins.

## 3. Training
Learning objectives, the format that fits (docs, workshop, pairing), practice, and a check that the skill transferred.

## 4. Change
For organisational change: why, what changes for whom, timeline, communication plan by audience, and how feedback is collected and answered.

Employment-law questions need a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
