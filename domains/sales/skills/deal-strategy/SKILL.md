# Deal strategy

Move a real opportunity forward with evidence about the buyer, not hope.

## 1. Qualify
Use a framework the team shares (MEDDICC or similar): metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria and process, paper process, identified pain, champion, competition. Mark each as known, assumed or unknown.

## 2. Prepare each call
Goal of the call, what you need to learn, hypotheses about the pain, questions in order, and the next step you will ask for. Research the account and attendees first.

## 3. Map the stakeholders
Who decides, who influences, who blocks, and what each needs to say yes. Plan how to reach the economic buyer.

## 4. Advance
Tailored demo or technical validation against their criteria; a proposal that restates their problem, the outcome, the plan and the price; objections answered with evidence.

## 5. Inspect the pipeline
Each deal has a dated next step and a stage backed by evidence. Forecast by stage exit criteria; flag stalled deals and what would unblock them.
