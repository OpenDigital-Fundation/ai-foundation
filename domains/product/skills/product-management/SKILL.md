# Product management

Decide what to build and why, write it down so a team can build it, and check afterwards that it worked.

## 1. Frame the outcome
State the user problem, who has it, the evidence (research, tickets, data) and the business outcome it moves. Write the success metric and its current baseline. No baseline means the first task is measuring it.

## 2. Shape the bet
Compare two or three options, including "do nothing". For each: expected impact, confidence and effort (RICE or ICE), risks and what would prove it wrong. Recommend one.

## 3. Write the PRD
Problem, goals and non-goals, users and jobs to be done, requirements as user stories with acceptance criteria, success metrics, risks, open questions, launch plan. In a repository, the PRD is the spec: hand it to `workflow:brainstorming` or `workflow:writing-plans`.

## 4. Prioritise
Rank the backlog by value over effort against the current outcome goal; cut or defer the rest explicitly. Re-rank when evidence changes, not when someone asks loudest.

## 5. Learn
Run experiments with a hypothesis, metric, sample size and stop rule. Synthesise feedback into themes with counts and quotes. After launch, compare the metric to the baseline and write what you learned.

Market, competitor and capability analyses are in the references.
