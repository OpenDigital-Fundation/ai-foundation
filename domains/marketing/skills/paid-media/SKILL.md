# Paid media

Spend where the next dollar returns the most, and prove it with clean measurement.

## 1. Measurement first
Confirm conversion tracking end to end (pixel or tag, server-side events where possible, deduplication, consent mode) and agree the attribution model before changing bids. Without trustworthy conversions, stop and fix tracking.

## 2. Structure
Campaigns by goal and audience, ad groups by tight intent, budgets by expected return. Search: match types and negatives from real query reports. Social and display: audiences, exclusions, frequency caps.

## 3. Creative
Several angles per audience, each tied to a specific pain or desire, with clear offers and CTAs. Rotate on fatigue signals.

## 4. Optimise weekly
Mine search terms, add negatives, shift budget toward cost per acquisition and return targets, pause losers with enough data, and test one change at a time.

## 5. Audit and report
Wasted spend, tracking gaps, structure problems and opportunities, ranked by money at stake. Report spend, conversions, CPA or ROAS against target and the next actions.
