See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Use this adaptation for evidence/research and evaluation artifacts, not patient-specific diagnosis, treatment, dosing, triage or live clinical decisions. Work from public evidence and synthetic or appropriately reviewed aggregate data; no raw patient records or hidden external uploads.

Record intended use, source versions, population, exclusions, transformations, uncertainties and review roles in a separate evidence/review record. For an outcome evidence profile, document bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication-bias judgments with source IDs and rationale. A qualified review process determines certainty; keywords, p-values or a structural checker do not. No upstream helper scripts or assets are bundled by this reference.
