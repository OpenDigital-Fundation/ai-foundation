# clinical-reports — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `K-Dense-AI/claude-scientific-skills` at `2d2065f`, `skills/clinical-reports/SKILL.md#non-negotiable-boundary,input-gate,safe-drafting-workflow,privacy`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Draft from a structured source-fact manifest instead of reconstructing a plausible patient story. Every populated fact, date, unit or supplied professional assessment maps to a verified fact ID. Preserve missingness, uncertainty and conflicting records; do not silently normalize or infer clinical meaning.

Choose the requested note/report scaffold, populate only authorized facts and run structural/consistency checks appropriate to the actual local tooling. No upstream report generators or validators are installed here. Keep source and draft separate; clinical, privacy, statistical and regulatory reviewers approve as applicable. Do not sign, amend a live chart, submit or claim readiness on another person's behalf.
