# Logistics operations

## Establish the shipment contract
Capture shipment/order IDs, route, goods/handling class, promised windows/timezones, parties, carrier, documents, contractual responsibilities and decision owner. Verify current facts instead of using generic liability, claim-deadline or refund rules.

## Operate and resolve
1. Track planned versus actual milestones with source and observation time. Separate carrier assertions, sensor evidence and confirmed receipt.
2. For exceptions, classify delay, loss, damage, shortage, refusal, misdelivery or handling breach. Preserve evidence and escalate safety-sensitive cargo to qualified operators under the applicable process.
3. Prioritize using actual safety, time, customer and financial impact. Set thresholds locally; example dollar limits, multipliers and generic claim windows are not policy.
4. Compare recovery options, ownership, remaining shelf life/capacity and authorized costs. Do not promise replacement, refunds, carrier removal or liability before approval.
5. Score carriers by defined service windows, denominator, sample size, claims and invoice accuracy; investigate evidence before attributing fraud or poor intent.
6. For returns, record authorization, inspection, condition and custody; choose permitted restock, repair, vendor return, recycling or other disposition from product safety and local policy before economics.
7. Communicate confirmed status, action owner and next update; close only with resolution evidence and reconciled inventory/credits.

## Handoff
An exception/return log, evidence links, approved resolution, next owner, status and improvement actions. No automated contract, payment, shipment or disposal action is authorized by this skill alone.
