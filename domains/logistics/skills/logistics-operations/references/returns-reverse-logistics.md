See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Record return authorization, item/serial, source order, condition, inspection evidence and chain of custody. Use category-specific functional and safety checks, not generic A–D grades as proof that resale is lawful or safe.

Choose permitted disposition before optimizing recovery value: restock, repair, vendor return, recycling or other authorized route. Check current recall, product-safety, data-removal, warranty and local requirements with responsible owners. Reconcile stock and credits and preserve vendor claim evidence. Refunds, write-offs, disposal and exception decisions require policy and approval; reject blanket tax deductions, mandatory destruction or customer-value rules from examples.
