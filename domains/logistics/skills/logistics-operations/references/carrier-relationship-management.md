# carrier-relationship-management — reviewed procedure

Adapted by OpenDigital from `affaan-m/ECC` at `b38be6a`, `skills/carrier-relationship-management/SKILL.md#carrier-scorecarding,portfolio-strategy,carrier-selection-for-new-lanes,when-to-consolidate-vs-diversify,carrier-exit-criteria`. See this domain’s PROVENANCE.yaml. This is a reviewed procedure, not an installed upstream tool or a validated runtime example.

Define carrier metrics by contractual window, observation source, period, denominator and sample size: pickup/delivery timeliness, tender acceptance, claims frequency/severity, invoice accuracy and recovery performance. Investigate data and operational causes before attributing bad intent.

Build lane coverage and fallbacks from volume, capacity, handling needs and correlated risk. Validate applicable carrier authority/insurance under the actual jurisdiction and contract. Use an approved trial and documented corrective action before portfolio changes. Source dollar amounts, score thresholds and fixed trial/exit periods are illustrative and are not adopted as defaults.
