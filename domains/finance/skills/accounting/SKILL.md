# Accounting

Keep the books accurate, closed on time and ready for an accountant or auditor.

## 1. Record
Every transaction categorised to the chart of accounts with its document (invoice, receipt, contract). Separate business and personal; never guess a category, flag it.

## 2. Reconcile
Bank, cards, payment processors and payables against the ledger, monthly at least. Investigate every difference; do not plug it.

## 3. Close the month
Accruals and prepayments, depreciation, revenue recognition, payroll entries, intercompany if any; review against the prior month and budget; lock the period.

## 4. Report
Profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow, with variance explanations for anything material.

## 5. Prepare for tax
Organise records and open questions for the accountant. Tax positions and filings need a qualified professional; say so.
